# Privacy Policy: DepEd School Portal

**Last Updated:** 2025-01-15  
**Language:** English & Tagalog (Pilipino)

---

## 1. What Information Do We Collect?

### 1.1 School Administrator & Staff Data
When school staff creates an account:
- **Name, email address, phone number**
- **Role** (Admin, Registrar, Teacher)
- **Login history** (when you logged in, what you did)

### 1.2 Student Data
Schools provide:
- **DepEd Student ID (LRN)**
- **Name, date of birth, gender**
- **Guardian/parent contact information**
- **Attendance records** (present, absent, tardy)
- **Grades and academic marks**
- **Class/section enrollment**

### 1.3 Parent & Guardian Data
When parents log in:
- **Email address, phone number**
- **Name, relationship to student**
- **Account activity** (what grades/attendance they viewed)

---

## 2. Why Do We Collect This Information?

We collect data **ONLY to provide the Platform services:**

- ✅ Show students and teachers their grades and attendance
- ✅ Allow teachers to mark attendance and enter grades
- ✅ Allow parents to monitor their child's academic progress
- ✅ Generate school reports (overall attendance %, grade averages)
- ✅ Maintain backup copies for data recovery
- ✅ Log all data access for security and compliance
- ✅ Comply with DepEd and National Privacy Commission requirements

We DO NOT:
- ❌ Sell student data to anyone
- ❌ Use student data for marketing or advertising
- ❌ Use student data to train AI models (without written permission)
- ❌ Share student data with third parties for profit
- ❌ Use data for profiling or predictive analytics on students
- ❌ Combine student data with data from other companies

---

## 3. How Long Do We Keep Your Data?

### 3.1 While Your School Account Is Active
- **Indefinite retention** — Your data stays as long as you want it
- You can request deletion anytime, and we'll delete within 30 days

### 3.2 After School Account Closes
- **3-year retention** — We keep data for legal compliance and audit purposes
- After 3 years, data is automatically deleted
- School can request earlier deletion anytime

### 3.3 Backups
- **30-day backup retention** — We keep daily backups to recover from data loss
- Backups are deleted automatically after 30 days
- Schools can request additional backup retention (paid service)

## 4. Your Rights (Data Privacy Act of 2012)

Under Philippine law, you have the right to:

### 4.1 Right to Access
- **Request:** Ask us for all data we hold about you
- **Timeline:** We respond within 15 days
- **Format:** Data provided as CSV or PDF

### 4.2 Right to Correction
- **Request:** If your data is wrong (name misspelled, wrong grade), we'll fix it
- **Timeline:** Within 15 days

### 4.3 Right to Deletion
- **Request:** Ask us to delete your personal data
- **Exception:** If DepEd law requires us to keep records, we may retain for legal reasons
- **Timeline:** Within 30 days (or within 90 days for large datasets)

### 4.4 Right to Object
- **Request:** Object to how your data is used
- **Example:** "Don't use my attendance data for reports"
- We will stop the use, unless law requires us to keep it

### 4.5 Right to Data Portability
- **Request:** Get your data in a portable format (CSV, JSON)
- **Timeline:** Within 15 days
- **Cost:** Free

## 5. How We Protect Your Data

### 5.1 Encryption
- **At Rest:** All student data encrypted with AES-256 (military-grade encryption)
- **In Transit:** All data sent using HTTPS (secure connection)
- **Passwords:** Hashed with bcrypt + salt (we never store plain passwords)

### 5.2 Access Control
- **Role-Based:** Different users see different data
  - Teachers see only their class's attendance & grades
  - Parents see only their child's data
  - Admins see school-wide data
- **No Sharing:** Staff cannot access data outside their role

### 5.3 Audit Logs
- **Every Access Logged:** Who accessed what data, when, what they did
- **Retention:** Logs kept for 1 year for security audit
- **School Access:** Schools can request audit logs anytime

### 5.4 Data Center Security
- **Hosting:** Philippines-based servers (CALABARZON region)
- **Facility Security:** 24/7 monitoring, access control, backup power
- **Disaster Recovery:** Daily automatic backups, can restore within 1 hour

---

## 6. Who Has Access to Your Data?

### 6.1 Your School Staff
- **Teachers:** Can see attendance & grades for their classes only
- **Admins/Registrar:** Can see all school data
- **Principals:** Can see reports but not individual student details

### 6.2 Parents & Guardians
- Can view their own child's data only
- Cannot see other students' information

### 6.3 Students
- Can view their own grades & attendance
- Cannot view other students' data

### 6.4 Third Parties
- **We do NOT share data with third parties**, with these exceptions:
  - **DepEd (if required):** Only data legally mandated for DepEd reporting
  - **Hosting Provider:** Our data center stores encrypted data (they cannot read it)
  - **Legal Requirement:** If court order or NPC requires, we comply (with notice to school)

## 7. Data Breach Response

### 7.1 If We Discover a Breach
**We will:**
- Notify your school within **72 hours** of discovery
- Explain what data was exposed
- Tell you what we did to stop the breach
- Provide steps you should take

### 7.2 School's Responsibility
**School must:**
- Notify parents if their child's data was exposed (per DepEd/NPC guidelines)
- Change passwords and advise staff to do the same
- Monitor student accounts for suspicious activity

### 7.3 Reporting to Authorities
- If breach affects >1000 people or is high-risk, we notify **National Privacy Commission**
- If breach is from criminal attack, we may report to **Philippine National Police (PNP)**

## 8. Your Choices & Controls

### 8.1 What You Can Control
- **Change Password:** Anytime from your account settings
- **Update Contact Info:** Email, phone number
- **Export Data:** Download all your data in CSV format
- **Delete Account:** Close your account (admin/staff only; parents cannot self-delete)

### 8.2 What You Cannot Control
- **Attendance & Grades:** Teachers decide what to record (school data, not your data)
- **School Reports:** Admins decide what reports to generate
- **Data Retention:** School decides how long to keep data (3-year minimum per law)

### 8.3 Communications
- **Opt-Out of Emails:** You can unsubscribe from non-critical emails
- **SMS Notifications:** Schools enable/disable SMS alerts
- **Cannot Opt-Out Of:** Critical security alerts (password reset, login from new device)

## 9. Data Processing Agreement (School Requirement)

### 9.1 Your School's Role
Your school is the **Data Controller** — they decide what data to collect and keep.

### 9.2 Our Role
We are the **Data Processor** — we handle data on behalf of the school.

### 9.3 School's Responsibility
Your school must:
- Obtain **parent/guardian consent** before collecting student data
- Provide schools with our **Privacy Policy & Terms of Service**
- Ensure staff follows data protection practices
- Request deletion if parents ask
