## 1. Service Description

DepEd School Portal ("Platform") is a school management system for Philippine elementary and secondary schools. The Platform allows authorized school staff to manage student attendance, grades, and records, and allows authorized students and parents to view their academic progress.

---

## 2. Data Ownership & Use

### 2.1 School Data Ownership
**All student, staff, and school data belongs to the school.** We are a service provider and have no ownership rights over any data. The school retains 100% ownership and control of all data.

### 2.2 Prohibited Use
We will **NEVER:**
- Sell or share student data with third parties
- Use student data for marketing, profiling, or targeted advertising
- Use student data to train AI models without explicit written consent
- Share student data with government agencies beyond what DepEd requires
- Monetize student data in any way

### 2.3 Permitted Use
We use student data ONLY to:
- Provide the Platform services (display grades, attendance, etc.)
- Generate school reports (attendance %, grade distribution)
- Maintain audit logs and backups (for security and recovery)
- Comply with DepEd or NPC requirements

## 3. Service Level Agreement (SLA)

### 3.1 Uptime Guarantee
We commit to **99.5% uptime** on a monthly basis.

- Uptime = Platform is accessible and functional
- Excludes: Scheduled maintenance (announced 72 hours in advance)
- Excludes: Forces beyond our control (internet outages, data center issues)

### 3.2 Scheduled Maintenance
We will perform maintenance outside school hours (typically 2 AM–4 AM Philippine Time, Sunday).

---

## 4. Data Security & Privacy

### 4.1 Encryption
- All student data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption
- All data in transit uses HTTPS (no plain HTTP)
- Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and salt (we do NOT store plain text passwords)

### 4.2 Access Control
- Only authorized school staff can view student data
- Access is role-based: Admin, Teacher, Student, Parent
- Each role sees only data they're authorized for (e.g., parents see only their child's grades)

### 4.3 Audit Logs
- Every access to student data is logged (who, when, what action)
- Schools can request audit logs anytime for compliance purposes
- Audit logs are retained for 1 year minimum

---

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion

### 5.1 Active Data Retention
While your school account is active, we retain all data indefinitely (your data, your choice).

### 5.2 Inactive Account Retention
After a school closes or cancels the subscription:
- We retain all data for **3 years** for legal compliance and backup purposes
- School can request data deletion anytime (we will delete within 30 days)
- After 3 years, inactive school data is automatically deleted

### 5.3 Right to Deletion (GDPR/DPA Compliance)
Students (age 18+) or parents can request deletion of a student's personal data. We will delete within 30 days, unless:
- Deletion is prohibited by law (e.g., DepEd requires records for 3 years)
- School requests data retention for ongoing academic purposes

### 6.1 Data Export
Schools can export all data anytime:

### 6.2 Data Migration

All exported data is in standard formats without proprietary encryption. Schools can import their data into any other platform anytime.
